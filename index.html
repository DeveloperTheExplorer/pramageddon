HOW TO KEEP THE PEACE

01

Watch the prams

Little bubbles show what a stirring baby wants. Smokker and tepper wait on the porch. Flasker need warming on the stove. Bamser live in the chest inside.

02

Give the right thing

Walk close and press E to pick up an item, then E at the pram to give it. One pair of hands, one thing at a time. Q drops it, SHIFT dashes, and R throws it in the direction Inger faces. The ground arrow turns mint when you are aimed at a valid pram.

03

Handle the wails

A crying baby won't take a binky. Scoop them up with E, carry them inside, and tuck them into the big crib. Or aim at the crib and press R to throw the baby. A perfect throw is a shortcut; a missed baby ends the run.

04

Protect the peace

Crying drains the neighbourhood peace bar. The run ends at zero. New prams arrive, needs bite faster, and foxes get bold. Keep the loop moving.
