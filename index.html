HOW TO KEEP THE PEACE

01

Watch the prams

Little bubbles show what a stirring baby wants. Smokker and tepper wait on the porch. Flasker need warming on the stove. Bamser live in the chest inside.

02

Give the right thing

Walk close and press E to pick up an item, then E at the pram to give it. One pair of hands, one thing at a time. Q drops it.

03

Handle the wails

A crying baby won't take a binky. Scoop them up with E, carry them inside, and tuck them into the big crib. Wait for the shushing to finish, then carry them back to their own pram.

04

Protect the peace

Crying drains the neighbourhood peace bar. Keep it above zero until sunrise. Foxes are not helpful. Shoo them by getting close and pressing E.
